Preamble
CERN has developed the CERN Open Hardware Licence ("CERN OHL") to foster
collaboration and the sharing of open hardware designs. The CERN OHL provides a
legal framework for the release and dissemination of Hardware Designs and the
manufacture and distribution of Products based on them.

This version, the CERN-OHL-S (Strongly Reciprocal), is intended to ensure that
all modifications to the hardware design and derived hardware designs are made
available under this same licence.

For the full legal text of the CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 - Strongly Reciprocal,
please refer to the official CERN OHL repository at:
https://cern-ohl.web.cern.ch/
